# Time Off and Leave

We want people to rest. This page covers paid time off, sick days, holidays, and longer leaves.

## Paid time off

Full-time employees accrue 1.67 days of PTO per month, which adds up to 20 days per year. PTO requests of three days or more should be submitted at least two weeks in advance through the HR portal. Requests of one or two days only need a heads-up to your manager.

## Carryover

You can carry over up to 5 unused PTO days into the next calendar year. Anything above 5 days expires on December 31. Unused PTO is paid out when you leave the company, in line with local law.

## Sick leave

Sick leave is unlimited and separate from PTO. If you are out sick for more than 3 consecutive working days, please provide a doctor's note to HR.

## Company holidays

We observe 11 company holidays per year, including a winter break between December 24 and January 1. The full calendar is published in the HR portal each November.

## Parental leave

All parents receive 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave, which can be taken any time within the first year after a birth or adoption. The leave can be split into up to two blocks.

## Sabbatical

After 5 years at the company, employees are eligible for a 4-week paid sabbatical, in addition to regular PTO.
