# New Engineer Onboarding

Welcome to Kestrel Robotics! This page walks you through your first weeks.

## Before your first day

Your laptop ships to your home address about a week before you start. When it arrives, sign in with the temporary password from your welcome email and set up multi-factor authentication. Do not install any software until IT has finished the automatic setup, which takes about an hour.

## First week

On day one you will meet your manager and your onboarding buddy, an engineer on a neighboring team who answers questions during your first month. Your first week includes a safety training course, which you must complete before visiting any customer warehouse, and a tour of the test warehouse in Fremont.

By the end of the first week you should have the development environment running and have shipped a small change, usually a documentation fix.

## Development environment

Clone the monorepo and run `make bootstrap`, which installs the toolchains for Go, Rust, and TypeScript. The robot simulator runs locally and lets you test changes without real hardware.

## First 90 days

Your manager will agree goals with you for 30, 60, and 90 days. You join the on-call rotation after 90 days, starting as secondary for one week while shadowing the primary.

## Getting help

Ask questions in #eng-help. No question is too small during onboarding.
