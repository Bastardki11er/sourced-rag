# Performance Reviews and Promotions

This page explains how performance is evaluated and how promotions work for engineers.

## Review cycle

Reviews happen twice a year, in April and October. Each cycle includes a self-review, feedback from two to four peers chosen with your manager, and a manager review. Ratings are calibrated across teams by the engineering leadership group before they are shared.

## Engineering levels

The engineering ladder has seven levels, from L1 (new graduate) to L7 (principal engineer). Most new graduates join at L2 after an internship, or at L1 otherwise. L5 is the first staff-level position.

## Promotions

Promotions are decided during the review cycle. Your manager writes a promotion packet with evidence that you have been performing at the next level for at least six months. Promotions to L5 and above also require a review by the staff engineering committee.

## Feedback outside the cycle

Managers hold weekly one-on-ones with each report. Anyone can request feedback at any time through the feedback tool; requests should be answered within two weeks.

## Performance improvement plans

If someone is not meeting expectations, their manager first gives clear written feedback. A formal performance improvement plan lasts 60 days and includes specific, measurable goals.
