# Interviewing

Hiring well is one of the most important things we do. This page explains how engineering interviews work and what we expect from interviewers.

## Interview loop

An engineering loop has four interviews: coding, system design, a robotics or domain deep dive, and a values interview. Interns and new graduates do two coding interviews instead of a system design interview.

## Becoming an interviewer

Before interviewing on your own, complete the interviewer training and shadow two interviews, then be reverse-shadowed once by an experienced interviewer.

## Scorecards

Submit your written scorecard within 24 hours of the interview, before you read anyone else's feedback. Rate the candidate on the four-point scale: strong no, no, yes, strong yes. Write down specific evidence for your rating.

## Debriefs

The hiring manager runs a debrief within three business days of the final interview. A single strong no should be discussed, but it does not automatically end the process.

## Candidate experience

Every candidate hears back from a recruiter within five business days of their final interview, whether or not we make an offer.
