# Expenses and Travel

Spend company money as if it were your own. This page explains what you can expense and how to get reimbursed.

## Submitting expenses

Submit expenses through the finance portal within 30 days of the purchase, with an itemized receipt attached. Reimbursements are paid with the next payroll run after your manager approves the report. Expenses submitted more than 90 days late will not be reimbursed.

## Approvals

Any single purchase over $500 needs written approval from your manager before you make it. Software subscriptions must be approved by the IT team regardless of cost.

## Travel booking

Book flights through the company travel tool. Economy class is the default. For flights longer than 6 hours, you may book premium economy. Hotels should be booked within the nightly limit shown in the travel tool for each city.

## Meals and per diem

While traveling, meals are covered up to $75 per day. Alcohol is not reimbursed unless it is part of an approved customer dinner.

## Customer site visits

When visiting a customer warehouse, wear the company-issued safety boots and high-visibility vest. Rental cars are allowed when the site is more than 30 miles from the nearest airport.

## Home office

New employees receive a one-time $500 home office stipend for a desk, chair, or monitor. Everyone also receives $50 per month toward home internet.
