# Data Retention Policy

We keep customer data only as long as we need it. These retention periods are enforced automatically by scheduled deletion jobs.

## Telemetry

Raw robot telemetry is retained for 13 months, so that customers can compare the same season year over year. Aggregated metrics, which contain no per-robot detail, are kept for 5 years.

## Camera footage

Some robots record low-resolution camera footage for incident investigation. Footage is deleted after 30 days unless it is attached to an open safety investigation, in which case it is kept until the investigation closes.

## Application logs

Service logs are retained for 90 days. Logs must never contain personal data such as warehouse worker names or badge numbers.

## Deletion requests

When a customer asks us to delete their data, we complete the deletion within 30 days and send written confirmation. Backups containing the data age out within a further 35 days.

## Customer offboarding

When a contract ends, the customer can export their data for 60 days. After that, all of their data is deleted.
